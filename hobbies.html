David Vizgan

Chess

I am a club-level chess player, rated about 1800 FIDE with peaks over 2100 in blitz and rapid on chess.com. My grandfather taught me the game when I was three; I became obsessed in my freshman year of college. From 2021 to 2024 I ran 64: A Chess Podcast, interviewing Peter Svidler, Anish Giri, Peter Heine Nielsen, and Maurice Ashley, among many others, and reported on-site from an elite tournament in Berlin. Feel free to contact me if you’d like to play a game.
