David Vizgan

Chess

I am a club-level chess player, rated about 1800 FIDE, with peak ratings over 2100 in blitz and rapid on chess.com. My grandfather taught me the game when I was three years old, but I became truly obsessed in my freshman year of college. In 2021 I started 64: A Chess Podcast, interviewing notable figures from the chess world including Peter Svidler, Anish Giri, Peter Heine Nielsen, Maurice Ashley, Cristian Chirila, Ruslan Ponomariov, Evgenij Miroshnichenko, Dina Belenkaya, Jennifer Shahade, and Jovanka Houska. The podcast was a minor success over its three-year run, and I was fortunate enough to conduct on-site interviews at an elite tournament in Berlin in May 2023. Feel free to contact me if you’d like to play a game.
